Who operates RELAY

RELAY is the engineering brand of IT Service International, an independent IT consultancy registered in the Netherlands. Every engagement is contracted, invoiced and carried out by that company.

Background

RELAY’s principal engineer has spent years running and rebuilding IT in places where failure is expensive and help is far away. From 2019 that meant leading the entire IT & telecommunications function for a major upstream oil field in the Middle East: infrastructure, business applications, service desk, vendors and contractors at a remote, high-security site.

Since 2024 the work has continued as an independent consultant and project director, including IT continuity through a change of field operator: contracts, systems and support moving from one operator to another without interrupting production.

How we agree the work, protect your information and hand over the result.

Review samples · Version 0.1 · 7 November 2026. These drafts are for discussion, not an offer or signed agreement. Final terms are agreed before signing.

01 / Start with a brief

  1. Describe the task. Send your objective, environment size and preferred timing. Keep the first message free of credentials and sensitive exports.
  2. Protect the discussion. If confidential details are needed, agree and sign a mutual NDA first.
  3. Agree the work. Confirm deliverables, exclusions, fees, dates and acceptance criteria in a Services Agreement and Statement of Work.
  4. Prepare access and data handling. Agree secure transfer, authorised access and any required data processing agreement before sharing personal data.
  5. Deliver and review. Work to the agreed scope, document findings and complete the agreed handover.

03 / What we agree for your project

  • The exact systems and activities in scope, with explicit exclusions.
  • Deliverables, milestones, review criteria, fees and payment dates.
  • Rights to use the work, any support and an appropriate allocation of liability.
  • Confidentiality, secure transfer, data retention and any necessary DPA.

An NDA protects confidential information; it does not replace a data processing agreement. Where we process personal data on your behalf, the required arrangements are agreed before processing starts.

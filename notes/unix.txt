
RELAY TECHNICAL NOTES
=====================

SUBJECT: UNIX PROCESS PERSISTENCE
AUTHOR: n0hup / RLY-01

The nohup utility allows a process to ignore SIGHUP.

Example:

nohup ./worker.sh > worker.log 2>&1 &

This is not a guarantee of process survival.
The process can still terminate because of
other signals, application errors or system failures.

PRINCIPLE:
Reduce unnecessary dependencies.
Understand failure modes.
Design for recovery.

Connection lost. Mission continues.

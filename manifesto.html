THE RELAY MANIFESTO

RLY-01 / UNIX / SIGNALS / RESILIENCE

HOME PRINCIPLES IDENTITY

01 / BEYOND THE SESSION

In Unix, nohup allows a process to survive the termination of its controlling session.

It does not make the process immortal. It simply removes one unnecessary dependency.

n0hup represents this principle: understand dependencies, reduce fragility, and preserve continuity.

02 / THE RELAY PRINCIPLE

A relay does not need to be the source of the signal or its final destination.

Its purpose is to maintain a connection, transmit information faithfully, and extend the reach of a system.

Connection lost. Mission continues.

03 / FIVE PRINCIPLES

I. Continuity over visibility.
The system matters more than recognition.

II. Understanding over authority.
Real control begins with understanding.

III. Resilience over perfection.
Failure is inevitable. Recovery is engineered.

IV. Precision over noise.
Information has value when it is accurate and delivered with purpose.

V. Purpose beyond connection.
Losing a channel does not erase the objective.

04 / OPERATOR DISCIPLINE

Observe before acting.
Verify before trusting.
Understand before changing.
Back up before replacing.
Document before forgetting.

Autonomy is not freedom from responsibility. It is the ability to make sound decisions within known limits.

05 / THE OLD INTERNET

The Internet was once a network of personal spaces, independent machines, and communities built around curiosity.

This site follows that tradition: plain HTML, readable source, minimal dependencies, and knowledge shared without unnecessary barriers.

06 / THE OPERATOR'S PLEDGE

I do not need to be the loudest signal.
I intend to be the one that gets through.

I value competence over appearance, clarity over complexity, and reliability over promises.

When communication fails, I preserve direction. When systems fail, I seek recovery. When conditions change, I adapt.

The session ends. The purpose does not.

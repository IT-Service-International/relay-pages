Engineering team

Fourteen engineers with about 300 years of combined experience in enterprise IT and industrial telecoms, most of it in oil and gas and other environments where downtime is expensive. Each works under a call sign; full CVs are shared with clients in proposals.

Who we are

The team has operated the IT and telecom estate of a major upstream oil field in the Middle East: 7,000+ users, around 1,000 virtual machines, multiple Active Directory domains, 350+ km of fibre, TETRA radio and IP telephony for 2,000+ subscribers. It has also delivered telecoms for EPC projects in oil, gas, pipelines and Arctic LNG.

  • Microsoft core infrastructure: Active Directory, DNS/DHCP, Group Policy, PKI, Exchange and Exchange Online, SCCM/MECM, SCVMM, SQL Server.
  • Virtualization, storage and backup: Hyper-V and VMware clusters, Huawei, Dell EMC and HPE storage and SAN fabrics, Veeam, MS DPM and tape libraries.
  • Network and security: CCIE-level Cisco campus, wireless, ISE and SD-WAN; Check Point, Darktrace and the Microsoft security stack (Defender, Sentinel, Purview).
  • Industrial telecoms: fibre construction, splicing and commissioning; TETRA and marine VHF radio; IP telephony and contact centres; PA/GA; videoconferencing, CCTV and IPTV; telecom design and as-built records.
  • Leadership and resilience: IT strategy, operator transitions, disaster recovery, ITIL-based operations, SLA recovery and monitoring.

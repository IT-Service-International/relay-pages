Engineering team

Six infrastructure engineers with a combined 120+ years in enterprise IT, most of it in oil and gas and other environments where downtime is expensive. Every engineer has run production Microsoft infrastructure at scale.

Who we are

The team has operated the IT estate of a major upstream oil field in the Middle East: 7,000+ users, around 1,000 virtual machines, multiple Active Directory domains and large SAN/NAS platforms. Three engineers have led infrastructure teams; one led an Active Directory and Exchange migration for 10,000+ users with no critical business disruption, and another delivered Exchange for 6,000 users.

  • Microsoft core infrastructure: Active Directory, DNS/DHCP, Group Policy, PKI, Exchange 2010–2019 and Exchange Online, SCCM/MECM, SCVMM, SQL Server.
  • Virtualization and storage: Hyper-V and VMware clusters, Huawei, Dell EMC, HPE 3PAR/Synergy storage and SAN fabrics, including a live migration to new storage with minimal business downtime.
  • Backup and recovery: Veeam Backup & Replication, MS DPM and tape libraries in production.
  • Hybrid cloud: Microsoft 365, Entra ID and Azure hybrid infrastructure.
  • Operations discipline: ITIL-based incident and change management, runbooks, SLA recovery and monitoring with Zabbix and Grafana.

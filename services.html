04 / CLEAR TERMS BEFORE WORK STARTS

Every proposal defines scope, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, fees and acceptance criteria. Licensing, hardware, travel and third-party costs are identified separately where applicable.

Assessments use read-only access and evidence review by default. Configuration changes, restore operations and failover tests require explicit scope and approval. Technical recovery exercises use an agreed environment, change window, rollback or failback procedure and stop conditions. Recovery objectives are targets; achievement is reported only where verified by the agreed tests. Support hours and response targets require a separate agreement; these packages do not include a default 24/7 service.

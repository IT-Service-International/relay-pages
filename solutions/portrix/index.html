01 / THE PROBLEM

When a process lives in email, its status disappears.

Approvals get buried in threads. Deadlines must be chased manually. Policies end up in disconnected folders. A structured workflow makes the current stage, owner and next action visible.

The goal is not more software. It is less operational uncertainty.

02 / CAPABILITIES

A connected workspace for routine decisions.

Based on the Portrix solution concept for Microsoft SharePoint Server.

[01]

Document workflows

Sequential and parallel approvals, comments, revisions, decisions and delegation within a document's context.

[02]

Tasks & deadlines

Personal task views, assignments, overdue indicators and scheduled email reminders.

[03]

Knowledge base

Approved policies and instructions, searchable collections and SharePoint-based permissions.

[04]

Editors for forms, fields, workflow stages, notifications, document templates and personal views.

[05]

Administration

A central settings panel for configuration, site objects, access review and diagnostics.

[06]

Document generation

Populate corporate Word templates with document fields and tabular records.

03 / DEPLOYMENT

Your process. Your infrastructure.

Portrix is described as a server-based solution for SharePoint Server Subscription Edition and SharePoint Server 2019. Staff access it through a browser. Documents and data remain on the organisation's servers, with access governed by SharePoint permissions.

Infrastructure requirements, compatibility, licensing and the security model must be reviewed for each deployment. This concept does not imply support for SharePoint Online.

05 / RELAY PERSPECTIVE

Continuity over visibility.
Precision over noise.

We approach business-process automation as an operational discipline: understand the work, reduce ambiguity, document ownership and build for supportability. The first conversation is about your process, not a feature checklist.
